## Summary

Revises the docstrings and documents under the `clear-prose` skill, writes the missing
public docstrings, and fixes the standard they are written to. The work runs in rounds
(roadmap Phase P), one commit each, reviewed before the next starts. This round, P0, lays
down the standard: ADR-0018 (the module docstring is the map, a function docstring is the
contract, the reasoning lives in the ADRs; one bibliography), the two new sections of
`docs/coding-standards.md`, `docs/references.md` with every citation in the tree
normalized to the `(Key, locator)` form and the five per-document reference lists
removed, `scripts/check_references.py` in `prek` and CI, and ruff `D100`–`D103` with the
not-yet-reached modules listed in `per-file-ignores`. Round P1 (second commit) revises
every docstring in `systems/` and `analysis/ode/auto/` to the standard and writes the
30 public docstrings that were missing; round P2 (third commit) does the same for
`analysis/`, where the two longest module docstrings in the tree lived; round P3 (the
fifth commit, after a correction to this document) covers `data/`, `training/` and
`experiment/`, and fixes the header form of P1 and P2. No code changes in any round.

## Design

- **ADR-0017 stays free.** `docs/roadmap.md` O7 and ADR-0016 § Consequences already name
  ADR-0017 for the `auto-isochrons` branch, so this branch's ADR is 0018 and the index
  shows 0017 as reserved. *Rejected*: taking 0017 and renumbering the roadmap's mention,
  since numbers are allocated when a branch is named.
- **The frontier lives in `per-file-ignores`, by directory.** Each round's commit deletes
  its directories from the list, so the check is green on every commit and the list is
  the current state of the pass. *Rejected*: one global switch at the end, which would
  leave 388 violations (181 methods, 168 functions, 30 classes, 9 modules, measured with
  ruff 0.16.9 on this base) unenforced until P5.
- **`check_references.py` scans running text, not only parentheses.** A bare `Lust 2001`
  in a sentence must resolve too; a form such as `Dieci–Russell–Van Vleck 1997` is caught
  because its tail `Vleck 1997` does not resolve. Uncited entries are reported, not
  failed: the bibliography may carry a work that a document names only in its
  `sources:` line.
- **Round plan** (roadmap Phase P): P1 the `symbolic-systems` modules; P2 `analysis/`;
  P3 `data/`, `training/`, `experiment/`; P4 `model/`; P5 `tests/`; P6 the documents,
  full revision for ADRs, `architecture.md` and `coding-standards.md`, paragraph and word
  passes for the design documents; P7 `normal-forms.md` alone. The `phase` branch's
  `analysis.md` §4–5 is revised when `phase` is reviewed.
- **The bibliography's open questions are in the file, not here.** Compiling it found
  citations in the tree that disagree with the papers (Namura et al. 2022's fourth
  author; Langfield et al. 2025 cited as §3.3 and §4.1 for the same model; two Gengel &
  Pikovsky papers cited as one; an unverifiable author list for Zielinski et al. 2014).
  `references.md` § Open questions lists them with the reading each entry took, so the
  list travels with the bibliography; the texts that cite them are not changed in this
  round beyond the Gengel & Pikovsky referent, which was ambiguous as written.
- **One-argument methods carry no `**Arguments:**` list** (P1, applied to
  `log_growth_rate(r)`, `phase(u)`, `isostable(r)` and their kind). When the opening
  sentence names the argument in the formula it computes, a bullet "`r`: the radius"
  below it states nothing the sentence did not; the list is kept where there are two
  or more arguments, a shape to state, or a default to explain (`isochron`, `flow`,
  `write_problem`). This is a reading of the standard rather than its text; Open issues
  asks whether to write it in.
- **Overrides get a one-line docstring with their formula.** ruff `D102` flags an
  overriding method as undocumented, and the base method's contract does not say what
  the override computes, so `HopfNormalForm.phase_shift` reads "$h(r) = c\ln r$"
  and `WinfreeNormalForm.basin_radii` reads "$(\max(a, 0), \infty)$". The alternative,
  `ignore-decorators` for `@override`, would need the decorator added to every
  override and would lose the formulas.
- **Nothing was moved into a document in P1.** Every sentence cut from a module
  docstring was checked against `normal-forms.md` (§3.1–3.3, §4.2, §6.1) and ADR-0013
  §5 / ADR-0016 §3–5 before it was cut; all of it was already there. The one exception
  is a fact that was in no docstring either: `period()` is signed, negative for a
  clockwise cycle (`normal_forms/base.py:169` divides by `omega()`, which is $-1$ for
  Winfree), now stated on the method.
- **P2 moved nothing into a document either.** The 70-line `floquet.py` docstring was
  the expected source of ADR amendments, but ADR-0015 §1–2 and `analysis.md` §3 turned
  out to hold every sentence of it, including the trace remark and the quoted Dieci et
  al. 1997 claim that P0 had predicted were missing (they are at
  `0015-floquet-by-seeded-periodic-schur.md:66-72` and `analysis.md:287`). The
  P0 change document's prediction was wrong because it was made before the two
  documents were read; the Open questions row below corrects the ADR-0018 context,
  which repeats it.
- **Headers on their own line (P3).** P1 and P2 wrote `**Returns:** The state, ...`
  inline; the review asked why `**Arguments:**` had a blank line and `**Returns:**` did
  not. diffrax writes every header on its own line with a blank line before the content
  (115 occurrences of `**Returns:**` in its source), and the standard names that
  convention, so the inline form was a deviation. Fixed by one pass over the 64 sites,
  each paragraph rewrapped at the header's indent; the standard now states the form.
- **Per-field strings become `**Attributes:**` lists in P3 too** (`SamplingSpec`,
  `SolveSpec`, `Provenance`, `Evaluator`), per the P0 decision; the strings carried the
  only documentation of those fields, so each list was written from them.
- **The numbered reference blocks of `model/invertible/` are gone (P4).** The 13
  `[1]`/`[2]` entries at HEAD (`polar.py`, `analytic.py`, `splines/*.py`,
  `coupling.py`) were the only citations the checker could not see; each is now a key,
  and the three works without an entry got one. `Gregory & Delbourgo 1982` is recorded
  through Durkan et al. 2019 and marked unverified, as the P0 entries without a DOI
  are. The alternative, keeping the blocks and teaching the checker their form, was
  rejected: two citation forms for one bibliography.
- **The class docstring carries the constructor's arguments (P4).** `CouplingLayer`,
  `PolarCouplingFlow`, `PhaseAmplitudeAutoencoder` and `TruncatedFourier` had their
  arguments on `__init__` or nowhere; each now has one `**Arguments:**` list on the
  class, as equinox writes them and as the P0 decision says, and the constructor
  docstrings are removed rather than duplicated.
- **The module `base.py` keeps both amplitude normalizations in one sentence.** The
  conjugacy model's Wilson–Moehlis $\Psi$ and the autoencoder's scale-free $Y_3$ are
  the reason evaluation fits a scale, so the sentence that states the contract has to
  name both; the alternative, a pointer to `normal-forms.md` §5.2 and §5.4 alone, would
  make the reader open the document to learn why the contract is loose.
- **A check for the header form (P4b).** The review found an inline `**Raises:**` in
  `experiment/run.py` after P3's transform; a search found 66 more, because the
  transform's paths were the two P3 packages and the P4 docstrings were written by
  hand in the old form. The rule is now `scripts/check_docstring_headers.py`, a regex
  on the four header names, in `prek` and CI; ruff's pydocstyle knows Google and numpy
  sections and not these, so a rule of its own was not an option. The alternative, a
  line in the standard alone, is what just failed twice.
- **`src/Isochron2PBVP` excluded from ruff and ty (P4b).** CI runs on `master` pushes
  and pull requests; the last three `master` runs all fail at `uvx ruff check src tests
  scripts` on that directory (54 errors; `ruff format --check` and `ty check src` would
  fail on it next, 23 diagnostics). An `exclude` in each tool's section is the smallest
  change; the alternative, listing the project's own paths in every CI line, would have
  to be repeated in `prek.toml` and in every editor.
- **Per file, in the rounds to come**: a reading pass (implementation, tests, ADR, change
  document, `git log -p`) before the prose pass; added statements are grounded in code
  read that session and cited `file:line` in the review row; a statement that cannot be
  grounded is a question in the table, not a sentence in the docstring.
