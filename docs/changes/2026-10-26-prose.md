## Design

- **ADR-0017 stays free.** `docs/roadmap.md` O7 and ADR-0016 § Consequences already name
  ADR-0017 for the `auto-isochrons` branch, so this branch's ADR is 0018 and the index
  shows 0017 as reserved. *Rejected*: taking 0017 and renumbering the roadmap's mention,
  since numbers are allocated when a branch is named.
- **The frontier lives in `per-file-ignores`, by directory.** Each round's commit deletes
  its directories from the list, so the check is green on every commit and the list is
  the current state of the pass. *Rejected*: one global switch at the end, which would
  leave 388 violations (181 methods, 168 functions, 30 classes, 9 modules, measured with
  ruff 0.16.9 on this base) unenforced until P5.
- **`check_references.py` scans running text, not only parentheses.** A bare `Lust 2001`
  in a sentence must resolve too; a form such as `Dieci–Russell–Van Vleck 1997` is caught
  because its tail `Vleck 1997` does not resolve. Uncited entries are reported, not
  failed: the bibliography may carry a work that a document names only in its
  `sources:` line.
- **Round plan** (roadmap Phase P): P1 the `symbolic-systems` modules; P2 `analysis/`;
  P3 `data/`, `training/`, `experiment/`; P4 `model/`; P5 `tests/`; P6 the documents,
  full revision for ADRs, `architecture.md` and `coding-standards.md`, paragraph and word
  passes for the design documents; P7 `normal-forms.md` alone. The `phase` branch's
