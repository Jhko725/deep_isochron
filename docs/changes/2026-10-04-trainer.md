# `trainer` — Phase C: the training loop, losses, schedules, evaluation, checkpoints

## Summary

The trainer becomes a small pure core with injected side effects (ADR-0009): `Trainer`
defines a jitted `train_step`, `TrainerState` holds everything that changes (now
including the loss-weight schedule's state), and `train()` takes a logger, a
checkpointer and an evaluation function. Losses are weighted sums of named terms whose
weights the trainer's schedule supplies each step — constant, curriculum, or Yawata et
al.'s once-and-for-all threshold switch. Validation is a fixed set of held-out windows
evaluated through the `AbstractPhaseAmplitudeModel` contract (`val/mse` selects the best
checkpoint; the learned period, κ, the bijection's round trip and Jacobian singular
values on the data's bounding box, and — when the data come from a normal form — the
phase and amplitude agreement with the exact chart). Orbax checkpoints save the whole
state and resume. The one-step-delayed logging survives as a `DelayedLogger` adapter.
`scripts/training/` is deleted (Phase D rebuilds it).

## Review round 1 (2026-10-04) — what changed in response

- **The loader owns the device transfer.** `train` no longer calls `to_device`; the
  trainer uses batches as yielded. `data.to_device(dataset, device, cpu_buffer_size,
  device_buffer_size)` wraps `grain.experimental.device_put` — the tutorial's option C
  ("`ThreadPrefetch -> map(jax.device_put) -> ThreadPrefetch`", "the recommended pattern
  for real training") — and the notebook uses it. NumPy batches still work (`jit`
  transfers them synchronously), which is what the tests do. This also covers most of C7:
  the CPU-side thread hides the window slicing whenever a step is longer than it.
- **Validation as a finite, deterministic dataset.** `validation_windows(source, length,
  stride)` enumerates every window at fixed starts of every held-out trajectory, in order,
  without shuffle or repeat; `Evaluator(val_data)` iterates it to exhaustion on every call
  (levanter's eval loop), accumulating sufficient statistics per batch — weighted sums for
  the MSEs, `Σ exp(iΔ)` for both orientations and second moments for the amplitude
  correlation — so nothing is held in memory and the bounding-box grid is taken from a
  first pass. Why not stream from the training-style loader: it is infinite and shuffled,
  so each evaluation would see different windows and `BestN` would select on noise.
  `collect_batches` stays as a convenience; the slow test and the notebook use
  `validation_windows`.
- **Context managers.** `Logger` and `Checkpointer` gain `__enter__`/`__exit__` (exit
  closes); `train` does `with log, ckpt:`, so the `DelayedLogger` flushes even when a
  step raises. (`typing.Self` is avoided in the protocols: the beartype import hook
  rejects it outside `@beartype` classes, as it did in `trainer.py` before.)
- **`terms -> (terms, aux)`.** `terms` must be exactly the weighted set (`__call__`
  raises otherwise; `__check_init__` checks `default_weights` against `weight_names`),
  `aux` holds diagnostics. `ConjugacyTrajectoryLoss`: terms `data`, `latent`; aux
  `final` (kept as a diagnostic — the error at the window's end exposes a frequency drift
  the mean hides). `PhaseAutoencoderLoss`: aux `omega`, `kappa`.
- **Typed schedule state.** `AbstractLossSchedule[S]`; `Constant`/`StepSchedule` are
  `[None]`, `ThresholdSwitch` is `[Bool[Array, ""]]`; docstrings name the state.
- **`circular_std`, justified** in `phase_amplitude_agreement`'s docstring and ADR-0009:
  offset invariance, wrap safety, and equality with the ordinary standard deviation for
  small spread (Mardia & Jupp 1999, as cited by SciPy's `circstd`).
- **American English.** 145 British spellings converted across `src`, `tests` and `docs`
  (`normalise_phase_plane` → `normalize_phase_plane`), the "centerd" typo from the
  search-and-replace fixed, and the rule added to `CLAUDE.md`.
- Hypothesis found a fragile assertion in `test_encoder_normalization` while the suite
  ran (`y = (−1, 2e−311, 0)`: `arctan2` lands on `±π` either side of normalization); the
  angle is now compared modulo 2π.

## Design

The decisions and rejected alternatives are ADR-0009. Points specific to this branch:

- **Why the delayed logging stayed** (Joon asked for the evidence). JAX returns from a
  jitted call before the computation runs; reading a metric blocks until it is done. The
  cookbook logs the *previous* step's metrics after enqueueing the current one, so the
  device never waits on the host. The host-side work that would otherwise sit in the
  bubble is the data fetch: our `windows(...).batch(128)` costs **15 ms per batch** (pure
  Python window slicing under the GIL; grain's 16 reader threads do not help, 14.7 ms;
  `mp_prefetch` failed to pickle the source in the dev container — roadmap C7). On CPU all
  logging variants measured 38 ms/step (nothing to overlap); on a GPU with a few-ms step
  the delay hides the fetch. Cost of keeping it as an adapter: zero in the loop body.
- **`default_weights` is `init=True`.** ADR-0004 asks for `init=False` static fields for
  `AbstractVar`s, but equinox warns about float leaves in `init=False` fields
  (`_MSG_FIELD_INIT_FALSE`); a tuple of floats is one. An instance may now override the
  defaults, which is useful anyway.
- **Protocols are `@runtime_checkable`** because the beartype import hook in the tests
  checks parameter annotations with `isinstance`.
- **`BestN` direction.** Orbax's `BestN(reverse=False)` sorts ascending and keeps the
  *last* `n`, i.e. the largest metric; minimization needs `reverse=True`. Found by the
  round-trip test (step 5 with `val/mse = 3.0` was kept instead of step 10 with `1.0`).
- **Restoring with sharding.** The abstract state passes each leaf's `sharding` into its
  `ShapeDtypeStruct`; without it Orbax warns and reads sharding from the file.
- **`Evaluator` collects batches once** (`collect_batches(loader, n)` → NumPy on the
  host), so every evaluation sees the same windows and the comparison across steps is
  meaningful; the bounding-box grid is derived from those batches.
- **Trainer loop never reads device values**: the step counter is a Python `int`, the
  float conversion happens in loggers (`to_floats`) and in `evaluate`'s return.

## Bugs fixed

- `ConjugacyTrajectoryLoss` averaged the squared error over coordinates as well as batch
  and time (a factor `dim` relative to the squared Euclidean norm the autoencoder loss
  used). Both now use `trajectory_mse` = mean over batch and time of `‖·‖²`.
- The previous trainer saved only the model's weights, so a run could not resume, and
  selected the "best" checkpoint on the training loss of the *previous* step.

## Tests

`uv run pytest tests/test_training.py` (≈40 s; the Orbax test writes to `tmp_path`).

`tests/test_training.py` (15 tests): building blocks on known inputs; `AbstractLoss`
weights the named terms in order and `default_weights` can be overridden; both project
losses are zero on the exact models (identity-bijection conjugacy on Bautin data; the
exact chart as autoencoder) and expose their terms; `Constant`/`StepSchedule`/
`ThresholdSwitch` (flips once, stays flipped; length mismatch rejected by `Trainer`);
`DelayedLogger` is one call late and flushes once on `close`; `PrintLogger` thins and
formats; a toy linear fit converges to the true `W, b` with a record every step;
`TrainerState` is deterministic in its key and respects the trainable filter; the loop
stops when the loader ends and evaluates at `eval_every` and at the end (`[3, 6, 7]`);
`evaluate` without `eval_every`, and no key, are errors; the switch flips inside the
jitted step and the logged weights follow; the float64 guard; `Evaluator` on the exact
models gives zero errors, the normal form's period/κ/params, round trip 0, singular
values 1, phase circular std 0 and amplitude correlation 1, and a wrong ω is detected;
`bounding_box_grid` and `circular_std`; Orbax save → restore `tree_equal` on the whole
state (schedule state and key included), best and latest kept (`[10, 20]`), missing
metric is a `KeyError`, and training resumes from the restored step; the Hopf baseline
through the trainer for 20 steps with evaluation.

Round 1 additions: `Evaluator` over `validation_windows(...).batch(4)` gives identical
results on two passes and zero error on the exact model; `eval_every=0` rejected;
`tests/test_data.py` — `validation_windows` enumerates every window trajectory-major, is
identical on two passes, defaults to non-overlapping windows, batches to `[4, 2]`, and
rejects bad strides/lengths; `to_device` yields JAX float64 arrays equal to the host
batches, in order.

`tests/test_baseline.py::test_phase_autoencoder_learns_the_hopf_phase` (`slow`, ≈70 s)
now trains through the `Trainer` with the once-and-for-all switch and asserts on the
`Evaluator`'s `phase/circ_std < 0.25` and the period to 5 %; it reaches a circular std
of **0.017 rad** (0.08 with B12's per-batch switch), period 3.146 vs 3.142, κ −0.57 vs
−2 and corr(Y₃, Ψ) 0.19 (reported, as before).
